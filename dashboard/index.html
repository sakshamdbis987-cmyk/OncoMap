RESEARCH QUESTION

How do selected breast cancer-associated gene-expression probes differ between normal breast tissue and breast cancer tissue?

BIOLOGICAL CONTEXT

Why This Research Matters

The selected genes connect to several themes often studied in breast cancer. These are useful ways to organize the question, not claims that one expression measurement explains a tumour.

01 / GENOME MAINTENANCE

DNA repair

BRCA1 and BRCA2 are associated with repairing damaged DNA and maintaining genome stability.

02 / SIGNAL TRANSDUCTION

Cell growth signalling

ERBB2, PIK3CA and EGFR are linked to pathways that transmit signals involved in cell growth and survival.

03 / CELL-CYCLE CONTROL

Tumour suppression

TP53 and PTEN are associated with cellular controls that can limit abnormal growth.

04 / HORMONE RESPONSE

Hormone receptor signalling

ESR1 encodes the estrogen receptor alpha, which is involved in hormone-responsive signalling.

STUDY CONTEXT

Methodology & limitations

05
DATASET

Public microarray study

NCBI GEO Series GSE42568, measured with the Affymetrix Human Genome U133 Plus 2.0 Array (GPL570). The series contains 121 samples: 104 breast cancer and 17 normal breast tissue samples.

PROCESSING

Processed measurements

The GEO2R results are based on GC-RMA processed array measurements. The table remains probe-level; the annotation file provides probe-to-symbol mappings, including some one-to-many mappings.

STATISTICS

GEO2R / limma

GEO2R uses the limma framework to estimate differential expression. Adjusted P-values use the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure to account for testing many probes. The dashboard displays the supplied results and does not rerun this model.

LIMITATIONS

Use as an exploration, not a conclusion

The groups are imbalanced, and clinical or technical factors may affect measurements. Multiple probes can represent a gene, annotations can be ambiguous, and these results need independent validation. No diagnostic or treatment decisions should be made from this project.
